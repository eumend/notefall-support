# Notefall support

Notefall is a free offline puzzle game for Apple TV by Eugenio Mendoza.

## Get help

- Email: hello@eumend.com

## Common questions

- **How do I play?** Left and right on the clickpad move the piece. Click to rotate; click and hold for half a second to hold it. Down drops faster, up drops instantly. Play/Pause or Back pauses. The How to Play screen in the game lists every control.
- **Can I use a game controller?** Yes. The Siri Remote is all you need, but controllers work too.
- **Where are my high scores saved?** On your Apple TV only. Deleting the app removes them.
- **Can I turn the music or the moving pieces off?** Yes, in Settings: music, sound effects, ghost piece and piece motion.
- **Does the game collect data?** No. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
